This privacy policy applies to the Earthquake Tracker & Alerts app (the "App"), developed and published on Google Play by StoDevApps, which is operated by Scott Obray as an individual ("we", "us"). The App is free to use, supported by ads, and offers an optional one-time Premium purchase.

Information the App collects and how it is used

Location

With your permission, the App uses your device's location to:

  • center the map on your area and show how far each earthquake is from you (calculated on your device);
  • show only earthquakes near you when you turn on the "Near me" filter. Your approximate coordinates are then included in the request the App sends to the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) earthquake data service;
  • create your earthquake alerts (see below).

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The App still works, but location-based features will not.

Earthquake alerts

To send you alert notifications, we store each alert you have in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). An alert record contains: the alert's name, center location (latitude, longitude and a coarse location code), radius, minimum magnitude, whether it is turned on, when it was last updated, an anonymous user ID, and your device's push-notification token. When you first allow location access, the App creates one alert centered on your location ("Near me").

The anonymous user ID comes from Firebase Authentication anonymous sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account login. The push-notification token comes from Firebase Cloud Messaging and is used only to deliver notifications to your device.

Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used, for example which screens are viewed, session length, app version, device model, operating system, and approximate country or region. This data is used in aggregate to improve the App.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and ad interactions to show ads and measure their performance. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ad Privacy Settings in the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium users are not shown ads.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is purchased through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. The App only records on your device whether Premium is active.

Information stored only on your device

Your settings (such as theme, theme color and default startup location), your Premium status, and simple usage counters used to decide when to ask for an app review are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

Maps and earthquake data

Maps are provided by the Google Maps SDK. Earthquake data is downloaded from the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program. As with any internet request, these services receive your IP address. When you use "Near me", USGS also receives your approximate coordinates as part of the request.

Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services, which handle data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services, Google Play Billing and Google Maps (Google Maps use is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service)
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Google AdMob
  • U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, so they can provide their services to the App, or when required by law (for example to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process).

Data retention and deletion

  • Alerts: stored until you delete them. Deleting an alert in the App (Alerts tab) removes it from our database.
  • Everything else we hold: to have all data associated with your anonymous user ID deleted, email stodevacc@gmail.com and we will respond in a reasonable time.
  • On your device: uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • Analytics and advertising data are retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Security

Data sent between the App and these services is encrypted in transit. Alert data is stored in Google Firebase, which applies its own security protections.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us at stodevacc@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Not an emergency warning service

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the USGS or any government agency. It is not an earthquake early-warning system. Alerts are sent after an earthquake has been detected and published, which can take several minutes. In an emergency, follow instructions from your local authorities.
